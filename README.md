# CS250-Portfolio

**How do I interpret user needs and implement them into a program? How does creating “user stories” help with this?**  
Interpreting user needs comes from meeting with them and having an open discussion about what they are looking for. If they are unsure of what exactly they needs, it's okay to start with a light overview requirement and start from there. User Stories help by breaking down these requirements in to smaller tasks, and applying a set of acceptance criteria to each. This helps teams tackle tasks together, and move faster.

**How do I approach developing programs? What Agile processes do I hope to incorporate into my future development work?**  
I approach developing programs by keeping code clean, keeping requirements clear, and always comunicating clearly with any team members I may be working with.

**What does it mean to be a good team member in software development?**  
Being a good team member in software development means staying a team player by always communicating your work, completing my projects on the communicated timelines, and being responsible for my mistakes.
